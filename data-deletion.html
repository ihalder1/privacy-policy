Data Deletion Request

To request deletion of your data from our application:

Option 1: Self-Service

1. Log into our application

2. Go to Settings → Account

3. Click "Delete My Account"

4. Confirm deletion

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to privacy@yourapp.com with:

  • Your registered email address
  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request"

Processing Time: All data will be deleted within 30 days.

Confirmation

You will receive email confirmation once your data has been deleted.
